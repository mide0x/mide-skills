# Skill dictionary

One line per skill on when to reach for it. Regenerated by `pick`, `add`, and `dictionary`. Do not edit by hand.

## /codex-first
Use it when
Have a clearly specified coding job, like a build, fix, or refactor, and want a cheaper assistant to do the typing while you design and check the result.

## /explain
Use it when
Staring at a piece of code, a type, or a design decision that still doesn't make sense and want it taught until it clicks, with a working example.
